HelpDesk AI Automation System: Knowledge & Retrieval

Overview

HelpDesk AI is an agentic system that investigates inbound IT helpdesk tickets against live identity state, decides what should be done, gets human approval before it deactivates, reactivates or deletes an account, and carries out routine IT service work. It was built by a 15-person engineering group split into five teams.

My Role

I was on the Knowledge & Retrieval team, which owned the grounded knowledge layer: every runbook, policy and manual the agents cite when they decide what to do. Retrieval is load-bearing in this system. If an agent can't retrieve the governing runbook section, it blocks and escalates instead of answering from general model knowledge.

Highlights

  • Sourced and structured three knowledge corpora (IT runbooks, policy, and a tool-access matrix; laptop manuals for 11 device models; and closed-ticket resolutions) into three hybrid-search indexes with filterable metadata.
  • Extracted vendor laptop manuals with Azure AI Document Intelligence, and made adding a new device model a one-file change by generating the downstream configuration and chunks from a single source.
  • Built a structure-aware chunker with stable chunk IDs, folding 63 heading-only chunks into their content and cutting sub-100-token fragments from 24.8% to 17.8% of a 966-chunk index.
  • Added heading context to each chunk's embedding while preserving de-duplication of shared manual text, which avoided roughly 36% more embedding calls on every rebuild.
  • Made device-model filtering mandatory on every manual query, so near-identical manuals can't produce a confident answer about the wrong laptop. Enabled multi-hop retrieval from runbooks to the policies they defer to.
  • Declared the corpus's distractor, out-of-corpus and near-miss topic lists that drive refusal testing, built the retrieval tools the agents call, and wrote an end-to-end retrieval test suite plus re-index checks.
